# The Last Signal: Winter Road

Start a fresh city from **Select Scenario → The Last Signal: Winter Road**. Saved cities from the previous two versions of The Last Signal keep their original chapters.

Two convoys are following the same guide rope into the crater. The first rescues are just the beginning: their survivors need a settlement that can house them, a winter reserve, and a way to bring down the people still on the ridge. The story ends with a decision about what the city will do with the road it built.

| Chapter | Deadline | What you do |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Two Bells in the Fog | 3 days | Admit the twelve outriders. Build a Medical Post for the school convoy or a Gathering Post and a landing reserve for the winch crew. |
| The Broken Winch | 5 days | Take the gravely ill and staff the wards, or take the engineers and build the supplies to rebuild the winch. |
| Who Gets a Key? | 5 days | Found the Public Ward Charter through warm homes and staffed services, or establish the Works Council through a working coal and steel district. Successful charters give permanent benefits. |
| A Road Off the Map | 5 days | Build a Beacon and bring back scouts, or lay a line of three Steam Hubs for the local guides. Both can survey a route for the evacuation. |
| The Locked Bins | 5 days | Coal works lose 35% efficiency during the dispute. Build a common reserve or staff independent coal works and a large depot. Success sends 160 coal to the next crossing. |
| The False Clearance | 6 days | Bring families down the open road or use the rope ferry to recover the work crews. A successful survey **and** Spare Rope project make a twin crossing available. |
| The City After the Crossing | 6 days | Put a Hothouse to work and cook fresh rations, or open a Factory and build two new automatons. Existing meals and machines do not count as new production. |
| The Long Night | 5 days | A break in the steam joint costs the city two insulation levels while the emergency is active. Pass six consecutive checks with the Generator on and the specified reserves intact, and meet the warm-home and operating requirements before time runs out. The Apprentice’s Machine project halves the heat loss. |

Deadlines start when you choose a plan. Completion spends the listed supplies once. If the Generator goes out or reserves dip below the listed amount during the Long Night, the next failed inspection restarts the six-check count. A missed chapter can cost its convoy, charter, or reserve; the story still moves forward.

The quest tracker shows the work each plan needs. **Operate** means open and staff the building so it can work. For warmth goals, use the temperature overlay: only homes rated **Liveable** or warmer count. For the scout route, build the Beacon, create a scout team, visit a Frostland site, then order the team back to the city.

The six optional projects are **The Spare Rope**, **A Room for the School**, **The Night Clinic**, **Keep the Signal Lit**, **Bread for the Landing**, and **The Apprentice’s Machine**. Each offers a specific job and reward alongside one of the main chapters. You can have one side project active at a time. The spare rope opens the twin crossing, school repairs insulate Bunkhouses and Houses, and the apprentice braces the steam joint. Other projects bring supplies or working families.

Opening the winter road at the end requires a successful crossing and a successful heat watch. It buys three shipments over eight days, including six engineers in the last convoy. You can also invest in permanent insulation for homes and clinics, or close the road and end the story without paying another bill. The city stays playable after every ending.
