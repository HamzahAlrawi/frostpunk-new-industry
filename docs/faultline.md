# The Last Signal: Faultline

Start from **Select Scenario → The Last Signal: Faultline**. A weather station with a dead telegraph taps its call sign through a buried steam main. Answering it means deciding which parts of your city can spare heat and keeping the Generator within reach of the next signal. The campaign uses Switchback Basin and its three Frostland salvage sites.

## Sending a signal

1. Choose a circuit at the briefing. Its heat and workplace effects apply as soon as you pick it. Build the required equipment and check the quest tracker for your first pressure band.
2. Keep the Generator running. Turn **Overdrive on** to raise stress. The valve checks every game hour and sends a pulse as soon as the gauge is within the required band. Each pulse vents some stress.
3. When the tracker calls for cooling, turn **Overdrive off** and leave the Generator running until stress drops below the listed ceiling. Then follow the next step. Completing the other city objectives while you operate the line makes full use of the time.

At **72%** stress, the circuit trips and affected buildings lose two heat levels. Let stress drop to **14%** to reconnect and resume the sequence. Completing the weld raises the trip threshold to **82%**. Jobs allow five to eight game days. Unanswered briefings select the stated plan after one day. A missed deadline carries the campaign into the next act and forfeits that job's reward.

## Decisions that carry forward

- Give homes and clinics a warmer first shift at the expense of industry, or give industry the steam and leave homes colder. A successful signal brings specialists whose bonuses last after the shift.
- Fit a pressure accumulator for faster stress recovery, or build an isolated bypass for warmer medical buildings. The accumulator unlocks a shorter, higher-stress finale. The bypass unlocks a lower-stress finale once a scout team has returned and the shelf survey is complete.
- Feed stranded shelters from local kitchens and earn a permanent Cookhouse bonus, or run the line at higher pressure while welders repair it and gain more headroom before a trip.
- Call shelters one at a time if you lack the refits for the other relay routes.
- At Broken Mile, hold three consecutive watches. Each watch must pass all its hourly checks, sends coal to the ridge, and leaves a minimum city reserve. During the leak, spend 35 steel and 60 coal on a two-hour repair at 38% stress or lower, or isolate the spur for twelve hours. The finished watches count toward the charter.
- A completed watch unlocks a permanent supply charter. The other plan moves the remaining families into the city and reuses the old pipe to insulate Steelworks and Factories. Each charter has its own city objectives and one outbound shipment.
- Complete the relay and sign a charter, finish at least five of the seven jobs, and keep trips to one or fewer to earn the **Basin Accord** and a lasting insulation bonus for homes and wards.

The first call, fracture, arrival at Broken Mile and dawn feature short camera sequences over the city. If a cutscene is interrupted, the next briefing can still arrive. Once the ending report appears, the city stays playable. An ongoing Faultline save can reach the new acts. A city that already saw an ending can start the expanded campaign in a new game. Existing [Winter Road saves](last-signal.md) retain their original campaign.
