## Faultline district heat and rescue

- Commission a heat loop for the clinic road or the works spur. The Generator and the actual temperature of completed buildings determine when the loop qualifies. The installed loop warms that district and shortens its Broken Mile route.
- Broken Mile convoys load automatically above the coal reserve. Heated stops govern departure and arrival. A convoy can wait through a cold spell without spending the load twice. A Coal Magazine still saves 20 coal per load.
- Lead with the clinic sledges or lift crew and manage two independently tracked rescue routes through an 18-hour whiteout. Choose which district gets the emergency boiler. Save either crew, or repair the lift beforehand and save both for the Basin Accord.
- Added tutorial cards, route requirements in the tracker, and reports for all four rescue outcomes.
