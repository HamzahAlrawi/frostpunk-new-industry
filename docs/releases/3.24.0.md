# New Industry 3.24.0 beta

- **Faultline now has seven acts and fifteen plans.** Keep the main running at Broken Mile through three fuel watches. A supply lapse resets the current watch. A burst seam chills buildings until you choose and complete a repair.
- The city and shelters settle their future through two different charters. Keep the ridge supplied, or bring its families to the basin and use the salvaged pipe to insulate industry.
- Four new in-engine cinematics stage the first call, the fracture, the watch and the ending with camera moves over your city. Briefings have a fallback if the sequence callback is interrupted.
- The main trips at 72% stress and reconnects at 14%. Finishing the weld raises the trip threshold to 82%.
