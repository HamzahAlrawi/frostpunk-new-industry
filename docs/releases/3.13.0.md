# New Industry 3.13.0 beta

Adds 12 laws in four choice trees. No new law requires research. Each tree starts
with an available root law, then offers two mutually exclusive follow-ups.

## Four new branches

| Tree | Root | Choose one follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community Care | Local Aid Societies | Neighbourhood Health Service or Critical Care Guarantee |
| Workforce | Trade Guild Charter | Master Craftsmen's Charter or Mechanical Maintenance Charter |
| Winter Preparedness | Winter Housing Code | District Hearths Ordinance or Protected Food Supply |
| Public Reserves | Communal Stockyards | Storm Reserve Mandate or Industrial Capital Charter |

Roots require no previous laws or technology and have a 12-hour cooldown. Each
follow-up requires only its root and has a 24-hour cooldown. The two alternatives
use Frostpunk's native exclusion-group mechanism; signing one rules out the other.
Scenario restrictions on when the player can sign laws remain in effect.

Benefits include faster medical treatment, reduced illness, housing and civic
insulation, larger heat zones, human or automaton industrial specialisation,
protected food workplaces, and substantial storage increases. See the
[complete laws reference](../laws.md) for exact values and four branch diagrams.

Open **Adaptation**, or **Administration** in The Last Autumn, and pan or zoom
outward. The trees occupy four outer areas around the native branches. The Last
Autumn uses camp, port and fabrication variants; Mechanical Maintenance becomes
Mechanised Worksites with safety and port efficiency benefits. Hope effects use
the existing native Motivation benefit in that scenario.

The four older researched charters retain their requirements, IDs and benefits.
All 112 native laws and the four original law books retain their original bytes,
registration order and unlock conditions. Native abilities and building configs
are unchanged. Existing difficulty configuration is preserved.

## Validation

46 automated tests pass. Owned-data validation decodes the actual law
prerequisites, checks that roots have none, and verifies parent and exclusion
groups, on-sign vectors, every new passive scalar and storage factor. Node and
connection checks cover the native laws, book centre and all new trees.

Removing additions reconstructs the entire original society configuration byte
for byte. Comparison with 3.12.1 also preserves all 120 previous law records and
all 825 previous modifiers byte for byte. The build checks 462 technology records, 869 modifiers, 43 recipes,
eight scenario graphs, all 17 starting profiles and native difficulty vectors.
The isolated install/uninstall round trip verifies all 33 patched entries and
exact restoration of all six archives.

No live game UI test was performed for these new trees. This remains a beta.
Close Frostpunk, uninstall the previous version and install 3.13.0; persistent
configuration and original backups are retained.
