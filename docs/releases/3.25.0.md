# New Industry 3.25.0 beta

- Seven native tutorial cards explain Faultline's pressure gauge, pulses, circuit trips, refits, scout surveys, fuel watches and charters. They appear when each part of the job becomes relevant.
- **Signal Relay** is a new day-one Tech building. It heats a small area at two coal per hour and lets the tripped main reconnect at 20% stress.
- **Coal Magazine** is a new day-one Resources building. Assign it to coal for 1,500 extra storage. A completed Magazine cuts each Broken Mile shipment by 20 coal.
- Both buildings have their own construction entries and reuse existing building models. Their main-circuit and shipment bonuses apply once.
