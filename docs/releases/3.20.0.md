# New Industry 3.20.0 beta

## Research balance

- Workshop Drafting Stations give +35% efficiency before Drawing Boards. The next three general Workshop steps give +30%, +25% and +20%. The automation step gives machines +35% and provides +2 Workshop insulation. The configured research duration and base Workshop curve stay the same.
- Steelworks upgrades cover tooling, feed handling, crews and casting. Continuous Casting grants +65% steel output, +3 insulation and +50% depot capacity for steel. Tool Libraries now improve timber production, while Pneumatic Mine Lifts improve coal production. The human and automaton industrial capstones still support either staffing strategy.
- Generator Draft Grates cut coal use by 12% and slow overdrive stress buildup by 10%. Later fuel and stress upgrades grant smaller, distinct benefits. In Steam Hub scenarios, Return Lines now come before Compact Heating Grids, so taking the final fuel-for-radius trade is optional.
- Preventive Field Medicine reduces cold-related sickness. Clinical research handles healing, insulation and Radical Treatment safety. Infirmary Recovery Suites also improves Houses of Healing where available. Food research gives insulation and output clearer roles, including fishing and kitchen upgrades in The Last Autumn.
- Narrow insulation and heating upgrades cost less. The opening Thermal Envelope costs more, and its later radius and sickness bonuses are smaller. The six Envelope stages retain their combined +7 insulation.
- Storage still has separate branches for local depots, bulk industry and storm reserves. Their capacity bonuses leave room for depot construction and placement to matter.

## Verification

The local build audit reads the serialized technologies and modifiers. It checks Workshop stacking, the casting capstone, fuel-saving limits, and the optional Compact Heating branch. The existing audits cover research reachability and layout, recipes, native configuration, and insulation tooltip totals. Campaign pacing and the long-run economy still need playtesting.
