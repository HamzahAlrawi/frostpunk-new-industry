# New Industry 3.9.0 beta

Adds 22 research definitions and nine production recipes built around medical
alternatives, resource conversion, workforce choices and specialised stockyards.
There are now 231 active research definitions across the supported modes.

- **Slow-Simmered Broth, tier 0:** a Cookhouse option that turns 2 raw food and
  6 coal into 8 standard rations in 30 working minutes. Conserves raw food while
  costing more fuel and kitchen time than Fuelled Stockpots.
- **Pressure-Buffer Boiler Drums, tier 0:** stress builds 50% more slowly, at
  the permanent cost of 20% higher Generator coal use, even with overdrive off.
  This is an optional, explicitly labelled trade-off.
- **Limb-Sparing Surgery, tier II:** removes Medical Post amputations caused
  by Radical Treatment while that law is active. Other injury and death risks
  are unchanged. **Core-Free Field Infirmaries, tier III** removes the one-core
  Infirmary construction requirement, offering another medical development path.
- **Condensate-Recovery Heaters, tier IV:** active Heaters use 50% less coal.
  Copies the native heater-activation conditions, preserving heat output and
  working alongside the native Heater Efficiency Upgrade.
- **Closed-Cycle Pyrolysis, tier IV:** replaces Retort Kiln Batches with a
  wood-saving process: 4 wood and 8 coal become 96 coal in two working hours.
  Net gain is 88 coal. Rapid Coking remains the faster ordinary production option.
- **Core-Charged Furnace Batches, tier V:** spend 20 wood and one Steam Core
  for 1200 coal in four working hours at a Kiln. **Artificial Spring Cultivation,
  tier V:** spend one core and 24 coal for 100 raw food in six hours, or 180 at an
  Industrial Hothouse. Both consume a core every batch and require renewable
  core-production research. Hothouses still obey weather shutdowns.
- **Neighbourhood Cache Networks, tier IV:** small depots gain 300% capacity
  for coal, wood, steel and food. **Polar Provision Vaults, tier V:** large depots
  gain 500% food capacity and 300% coal capacity. These give small and large
  warehouses different specialisations.
- **Winter Artisan Guilds, tier IV:** human industrial crews gain 150%
  workplace efficiency, with +2 workplace insulation. **Autonomous Maintenance
  Network, tier V:** automatons gain 150% efficiency at those workplaces.
  Neither research grants new automaton job permissions.
- **Riveted Assembly Platforms:** a scenario-specific Generator-construction
  upgrade for Endless Builders and The Last Autumn. It grants 100% workplace
  efficiency; the Autumn version also adds one safety level.
- **The Last Autumn** gets nine appropriate additions, including earlier
  condensate recovery and compact stockyards, medical alternatives, generator
  assembly, closed-cycle retorts, and Port Craft Apprenticeships. Far-Water
  Trawling adds a slower fishing option with better yield per bait ration.
- **On The Edge** gets nine additions suited to its available buildings.
  Generator, Hothouse and unavailable automation progression are excluded.

Production panels are checked at every relevant research combination and stay
within four simultaneously available recipes. Improved retorts replace their
predecessors rather than taking another slot. Factory recipe counts are unchanged.
Every supported research graph is checked for icon and prerequisite-line overlap.

Validation includes 23 automated tests, independent parsing of 430 technology
records and 786 modifiers, all 31 recipe prices/outputs/requirements and their
building attachments, and preservation of native ability and building configs.
Existing recipe records remain byte-identical except the two intentional retort
replacement requirements. Full archive installation and exact restoration
passed. The bounded local startup check loaded all six required configurations
with zero serialization or decompression errors; it did not load a save.

Close Frostpunk, uninstall the previous release, then install this release.
Original technology GUIDs and native abilities are preserved. This is a beta
balance update; new recipes and city strategies still need campaign playtesting.
