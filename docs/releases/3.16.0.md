# New Industry 3.16.0 beta — three new Endless modes

Choose **Restoration**, **Whiteout** or **Machine Age** directly from Endless,
beside the original three modes. Each has six automatic founding chapters,
two city doctrines and its own six recurring incident families. There is no
in-city campaign chooser for the new modes.

- Restoration develops a regional capital through civic or industrial choices.
- Whiteout adds four escalating thermal fronts and a sustained survival goal.
- Machine Age requires new machines, actual automaton assignments and a choice
  between core-funded automation or a human-machine partnership.

The founding chapters complete automatically through construction, research,
new manufacturing, staffing and operating conditions. Incidents and doctrine
upkeep continue afterward, with an optional quiet continuation.

**18 incidents and 54 responses** introduce heating emergencies, crew disputes,
rescues, fuel decisions, medical priorities and optional repair programmes.
Response windows range from **36 hours to five days**. Five manufacturing
programmes allow **four to eight days**, with explicit success and failure
consequences. Their countdowns appear in the quest tracker. Factory production
required by a programme counts new machines; existing stock cannot skip it.

The menu supplies six explicit card slots, short summaries and normal glyph
proportions. Card bounds include the decorative strokes. Engine reflection
metadata confirmed the layout property order; native orientation and alignment
enums are preserved. The nine-tab research bar is narrowed to fit Production.

Existing version 3.15 supply-dispatch saves retain progress and rewards. Native
story scenarios, research, recipes and laws keep their previous definitions.
Persistent configuration and original archive backups are retained.

## Validation and limits

66 public tests pass. Owned-data checks cover all six founding paths, 54 incident
choices, 18 response expiries, five repair successes/failures, funded-doctrine
payments, cached callbacks, temporary effects, late timers, mode isolation and
actual research-set membership. All native/mod research, law, tooltip, five-preset
and six-archive round-trip checks pass, covering 39 patched entries.

The final installed build passed a bounded startup check: all thirteen required
configurations and the edited Endless menu template loaded with zero
serialization or decompression errors. The check did not start a city or open a
save. Six-card rendering, live missions and events, and real save/load remain
**unverified**. This remains an experimental beta.
