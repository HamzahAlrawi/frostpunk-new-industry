## Faultline building art

- Rebuilt the Signal Relay as a telegraph tower with a brick equipment room, maintenance ladder, wired insulators and caged signal lamps.
- Gave the Coal Magazine three exposed loading bins with coal heaps, masonry supports and a snow-drifted barrel roof.
- Reworked Valve Works around a brick machine hall and an exposed pressure manifold with copper vessels, pipes and handwheels.
- Rebuilt Relief Dispatch as a timber loading yard with sledges, freight crates, a covered loading deck and a hoist.
- Sized the original models against the stock construction assets. The archive audit checks footprint, height, vertex and index bounds, draw ranges and registration. The Blender review renders the meshes decoded from the packed game templates.

The four buildings use their existing template and model IDs. See the [3.28.0 campaign notes](3.28.0.md) for the Frostland and mission changes. In-game renderer appearance and the city replay remain to be verified in a live campaign. Broken Mile still uses timed dispatch orders.
