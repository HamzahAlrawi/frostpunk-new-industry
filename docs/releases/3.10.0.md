# New Industry 3.10.0 beta

Adds 25 research definitions and 12 recipes, bringing the supported-mode totals
to 256 active research definitions and 43 recipes. A New Home gains ten additions,
The Last Autumn gains twelve, On The Edge gains nine, and Endless modes gain
eleven, including their shared additions.

## Faster research

Every mod-added perk now has **half its authored base research duration**,
including earlier releases' perks. A ten-hour research project becomes five base
hours before Workshop bonuses. Native technology durations remain unchanged.

Workshop base research output is also **25% higher than 3.9**, using a multiplier
of 1.5625 relative to vanilla. The native one-to-four-Workshop diminishing-return
curve is preserved, and the base rate applies with human or automaton researchers.
Existing Workshop research upgrades still apply. Production recipe durations are
separate from research times and have not been halved.

## New production and city choices

- **Core Fabrication Cells:** replaces the final Factory core recipe with three
  cores for 90 steel, 50 wood and 100 coal in ten working hours.
- **Closed-Loop Metallurgy:** replaces Deep Scrap Recovery with 120 steel for one
  consumed core and 30 coal in eight working hours.
- **Warehouse Recovery Lots:** On The Edge's Warehouse can recover 100 steel
  and 40 wood together for one consumed core and 20 coal in six working hours.
  It replaces Cutting Torches and uses the native multi-product blueprint format.
- **Steel-Assisted Pressure Kitchens:** two raw food, four coal and one steel
  produce ten standard rations in 30 working minutes. Replaces Fuelled Stockpots
  in cold-city modes; Autumn gets its own appropriate variant.
- **Timber-Substrate Growing Beds:** uses wood to conserve coal and avoid ration
  starters, with slower throughput. Replaces the heated growing recipe in both
  Hothouse grades; other growing choices remain available.
- **Reclamation Stockyards:** large depots gain 400% wood and steel capacity,
  while Factories gain 100% efficiency for human and automaton workers.
- **Protected Healthcare Heating / Industrial Heat Recuperators:** eliminates
  Heater coal upkeep at the specified medical or industrial workplaces. Native
  Heater unlocks, switches, power settings and activation conditions are preserved.
- **Compact Heating Grids:** an optional permanent trade-off. Heat-zone radii
  shrink 20%; Generator coal use falls 25% and Hub use falls 40%, or Brazier coal
  use falls 40% in the appropriate scenarios. Heater power is unchanged.
- **Recuperating Accumulators:** 100% faster Generator stress recovery and 15%
  lower Generator coal use, extending the pressure-buffer research branch.
- **Preventive Field Medicine:** before Drawing Boards, reduces cold-related
  illness by 25% and improves Medical Post healing efficiency by 75%.
- **Frostland Relay Workshops:** in Endless modes, 150% faster scouts and 75%
  greater Factory efficiency support recurring exploration and reclamation.

## The Last Autumn

New recipes connect the fabrication stations:

- Reusable Formwork: one exchanger and 18 wood become three profiles in two hours.
- Sacrificial Mould Casting: one profile, ten steel and 45 coal become two
  composites in three hours.
- Composite Exchanger Dies: one composite, twelve steel and twenty coal become
  three exchangers in three hours.

Further research adds core-free Machine Shops and Foundries, bait-free wooden
lures for fishing, faster forager routes paired with Cookhouse throughput,
worksite safety paired with medical treatment, medical and industrial Heater
fuel recovery, compact heating grids, pressure cooking, and early preventive care.

## Validation

The owned-build validator parses 455 technology records, 812 modifiers and all
43 recipes, including every output of the mixed Warehouse recipe. It checks the
actual halved research durations and four Workshop rate knots in the serialized
game data. Native research definitions, ability data and building configs are
preserved. Earlier production records are byte-identical except eight intended
replacement requirements.

All 26 automated tests pass, including mode eligibility, prerequisites, research
spacing, full-tier routing and a maximum of four visible recipes at each supported
production station. Full archive installation and exact restoration passed.
The bounded startup check loaded all six required configurations with zero
serialization or decompression errors, without loading a save. These checks do
not constitute a full campaign balance test.

Close Frostpunk, uninstall the previous release, then install this one. Existing
technology GUIDs are retained. This remains a beta gameplay/balance update.
