# New Industry 3.12.0 beta

Reorganizes the added research into five themed tabs: **Engineering, Industry,
City Life, Logistics and Production**. Each column follows one subject. Added
research has at most one non-tier prerequisite, drawn directly above it in the
same column. Hidden native and cross-tab research requirements are removed.
Native building unlocks still control which buildings can actually be built.

## Less clutter, stronger individual upgrades

47 smaller upgrades are consolidated into larger ones, retaining their effects.
There are 216 active research definitions across the supported scenarios, with
286 registered mod definitions retained for save identifiers. Examples include
combined industrial automaton actuators, core-saving construction standards,
medical treatment packages, sawmill opening tools and steelworks improvements.
Production recipes have their own tab and keep their normal building controls.
See the [research catalog](../research.md) and [consolidation table](../laws.md).

**Core-Saving Construction Standards** and **Core-Saving Port Construction** are
available at tier 0, before Drawing Boards, with no prior research requirement.
Each costs 24 wood and 10 steel and takes two base hours with default settings.
The cold-city package reduces core costs for Hothouses, Coal Mines, Wall Drills
and Infirmaries. The Autumn package covers Reloading Stations, Machine Shops,
Foundries and Infirmaries. Advanced grades can still have a remaining core cost.

## Four signable Civic Charters

Research the City Life civic branch, then sign these in the new **Civic Charters**
book. Signing uses an 18-hour law cooldown. Morale changes happen when signed;
the passive benefits remain.

- **Public Reassurance Charter:** Hope rises greatly; civic buildings gain
  +1 insulation.
- **Workers' Grievance Hearings:** Discontent falls greatly; Medical Posts and
  Infirmaries gain 75% healing efficiency for humans and automatons.
- **Rest and Recovery Covenant:** Hope rises moderately, discontent falls
  moderately, and cold-related illness falls 25%.
- **Storm Mutual Aid Charter:** Hope rises greatly, discontent falls greatly,
  and civic and healthcare buildings gain +2 insulation.

The Last Autumn substitutes an existing native Workers Council Motivation
benefit for each Hope effect. Discontent effects apply normally. All 112 native
law records remain byte-identical. No native abilities configuration is changed.

## Three additional thermal envelopes

All six envelope stages now occupy one continuous column, at tiers 0–5.
Existing envelopes retain their effects. The added stages are expensive:

| Stage | Tier | Cost | Additional effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IV | 3 | 420 wood, 320 steel, 3 cores | +1 insulation to all buildings using insulation; 35% less cold-related illness |
| V | 4 | 560 wood, 420 steel, 5 cores | +1 insulation to all buildings using insulation; 50% larger heat zones |
| VI | 5 | 720 wood, 560 steel, 8 cores | +2 insulation to all buildings using insulation; 50% less cold-related illness |

## Icons and validation

The three-core Factory batch now uses the native **Steam Core** resource icon;
its previous explicit override selected raw food. All 43 recipe output icons
are checked against their native resource coordinates. Research icons are
assigned by subject, including separate Autumn mappings, rather than inherited
from unrelated modifier donors.

42 automated tests pass. Owned-data validation covers 462 technology records,
825 modifiers, eight scenario graphs, every recipe output icon, the new laws,
native law preservation, five difficulty presets, all 17 starting-supply
profiles and 286 research duration offsets. The final startup guard requires
Society and Effects as well as the seven previously checked configurations.

This remains a beta. The bounded startup check passes with zero serialization
or decompression errors. In-city law signing and the new book's presentation
have not been interactively verified in this release. Earlier full campaigns
do not constitute testing of these new law features.

Your saved difficulty configuration is retained; the owner's installation stays
on easiest. Close Frostpunk, uninstall the previous release, then install this
one. Existing identifiers are retained, but a new city is recommended to assess
the reorganized progression; the installer does not edit or refund saves.
