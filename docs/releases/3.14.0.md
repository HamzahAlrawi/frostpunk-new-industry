# New Industry 3.14.0 beta

Fixes empty building modifier tooltips and consolidates repeated insulation
sources without reducing their bonuses.

## Readable source descriptions

All 326 mod-added modifiers now have nonempty names and descriptions. Building
heat and efficiency breakdowns can identify the relevant research or signed law,
including human and automaton benefits, civic policies and nested law modifiers.
The text is included in all 13 language tables, in English like the other mod
content. Research descriptions remain available in the technology tree.

## Fewer repeated heat rows

Six insulation families use one cumulative source per affected building:

- Citywide Thermal Envelope: +1, +2, +3, +4, +5, then +7 insulation.
- Healthcare Insulation: +1, then +3 from its two added late stages.
- Hothouse Insulation: +1, then +3 from its two added late stages.
- Cookhouse Insulation: +1, then +3.
- Housing Insulation: Houses +1, then +2; the final stage also gives Bunkhouses +1.
- Army Warehouse Insulation: +1, then +2.

Research nodes, costs and prerequisites are unchanged. Extra efficiency, illness
and heat-zone-radius benefits remain at their existing stages. Native modifier
records and law books are preserved. All existing identifiers are retained.
See the [tooltip reference](../tooltips.md) for the exact scope of each family.

## Validation

50 automated tests pass. The owned-data validator decodes the actual tooltip and
condition fields, checks every localized text reference, and verifies unchanged
heat totals and one active source across 133 scenario/research states. It also
verifies that every unrelated modifier field is preserved. The predicate format
is pinned to shipped native game-event records; no executable or ability config
is patched.

The build checks 462 technology records, 869 modifiers, 43 recipes, all law
branches, eight scenario graphs and all difficulty settings. The isolated archive
install/uninstall check verifies all 33 patched entries and exact restoration of
six archives. The Windows download and its embedded data are verified separately.
Direct comparison with 3.13.0 confirms all 869 modifier IDs are retained and all
19 other non-localization payloads are byte-identical, including research,
recipes, laws, difficulty, scenarios and building templates.

The new tooltip layout has not been checked live in-game. This remains a beta.
Persistent difficulty configuration and original archive backups are retained.
