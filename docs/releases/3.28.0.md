- Frostland now uses the shipped scenario's site-placement group. The Beacon reveals nearby scouting routes. Start a new Faultline city to initialize the revised Frostland and mission state.
- Replaced repeated stress sequences with a single signal on the opening and accumulator routes. The lake test shows hours recorded and keeps progress during interruptions. Infrastructure goals count completed buildings even when a shift ends or a store fills up.
- Dispatch orders confirm cargo, departure and travel time. Broken Mile counts three deliveries and interrupts the route for an emergency repair. Valve Works parts are spent in commissioning. Relief Dispatch cargo is spent on the chosen evacuation.
- Added four original building meshes with a build-time Liquid Engine mesh compiler. The Signal Relay now handles communications and circuit recovery. It has no Steam Hub heat zone.
- Shortened scenario and choice labels. The Generator replaces the buried camera anchors in six captioned sequences. Each ending leads into Frostpunk's city recording and replay system.

## Diagnostics

- `builder/inspect_faultline_run.py` reads the campaign variables in a supplied Steam save and counts matching events in an optional console log. It leaves the save file intact.
- `builder/preview_faultline_models.py` renders the compiled meshes in background Blender. The archive audit validates vertex and index bounds, normals, draw ranges, model references, and native registrations.
- Automated checks exercise 19 plans, timed deliveries, all three endings and the native replay handoff. Live scenario visuals, exploration and replay playback still need a campaign run. Dispatches use timed mission orders with an arrival counter.
