# New Industry 3.26.0 beta

- The five presets now control mod-added research duration, Workshop research output, gathering and production rates, and eligible bonuses from added upgrades and laws.
- Easiest sets mod-added research to 35% of its authored duration and Workshop output to ×1.85 vanilla. It applies ×1.15 to covered production rates, ×1.25 to scalable research bonuses and ×1.30 to scalable law benefits.
- Law power also changes the immediate Hope, Discontent, and Motivation effects of added laws. Preset-scaled descriptions label the Standard figures and the applied factor.
- Standard retains the existing balance. Existing JSON overrides still take precedence, and apply rebuilds from verified archives.
