# New Industry 3.14.1 beta

Fixes the separated half-circle choices in the new law branches and brings the
branches closer to the native tree.

Frostpunk renders mutually exclusive laws as two halves of one circle. The mod
had positioned the alternatives as separate nodes, pulling those halves apart.
All four choice pairs now share the same coordinate and parent in both
Adaptation and Administration, matching the 13 original native choice groups.

The new branch roots and choices use a smaller area: their vertical extent is
now -10 to +10 grid units, previously -16 to +16. Root connections are shorter,
and follow-up connections are at most five grid units. Native laws and the four
older researched charters keep their original positions.

Law identifiers, signing progress, prerequisites, exclusions, cooldowns and
benefits are retained. Research, recipes, cumulative source tooltips and
difficulty settings are unchanged.

## Validation

53 automated tests pass. The binary validator checks actual split-circle
coordinates against all 13 native examples, verifies all eight added pairs
across the two books, and checks compact node placement, connection lengths,
native-node clearance and crossing added connections. Regression tests reject
separated halves, unrelated overlap and excessively distant nodes or links.

The other build, tooltip, configuration and archive checks pass, including all
33 patched entries and exact restoration of six archives. No live game UI check
was performed for this patch; it remains beta.

Direct comparison with 3.14.0 confirms that only the x/y coordinates of 24 added
law records changed. All 32 other payloads are byte-identical.

Persistent configuration and original archive backups are retained.
