## Faultline and Winter Road map loading

Frostpunk could crash while opening a new Switchback Basin scenario. The crash log showed that the city loaded and the engine failed while reading the edited Frostland scene. This hotfix restores the original Frostland scene structure and uses the game's own site creation and reveal effects to chart the three salvage locations after the Beacon is built. The Switchback Basin city layout and the two mission buildings from [3.27.0](3.27.0.md) remain available.

All 70 tests and the scenario audits passed. The audit checks the entire Frostland object stream against the original and simulates site creation, reveal and reload. The install-and-restore check verified 51 patches and ten archive hashes. Live testing reached the game window. Loading into the scenario and sending scouts still need a live check.
