## Faultline mission buildings

- Added the **Valve Works** and **Relief Dispatch** to Faultline's Tech construction tab. Each has a dedicated two-order panel with native queue controls, a distinct interior, material costs, tooltips and a tutorial. Both buildings reuse the Factory's exterior model.
- **The Line Under the Lake** adds a choice between isolating the main or restoring the ridge lift. Fabricate the required parts and manage a continuous pressure test with coal held in reserve.
- **The Last Convoy** adds clinic and lift rescue routes with different equipment, city targets, arrivals and lasting benefits. Repairing the bypass unlocks the lift route. The clinic route is available after a missed repair deadline.
- Faultline now has nine acts and 19 plans. Unfinished Faultline saves can advance into the new chapters. Cities that already reached an ending can see the chapters in a new run.
- The installer now reads original donor templates consistently when a release both updates a donor and adds buildings based on it.

## Validation

The 70 automated tests passed. Serialized assets and decoded mission simulations cover both new queues, all 19 plans, expired deadlines, route unlocks and the three endings. An install-and-restore check verified 51 patches and all ten archive hashes. The deployed Windows Steam build loaded all 16 required configurations in a short startup check. The new chapters and their panels still need an in-game playthrough.
