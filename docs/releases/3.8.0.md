# New Industry 3.8.0 beta

Research cleanup and a revised progression curve: opening-game tools arrive
earlier, while late-game investments support sustained production and storms.

- Removed all seven added scout, Outpost and forager team-capacity perks.
  Vanilla team research remains available. Old mod definitions and GUIDs remain
  registered, with their added team capacity set to zero. Existing teams are not
  forcibly deleted and save files are not edited.
- Curated repeated upgrades into distinct effect packages. Examples include
  warmed irrigation, recovery suites, protected fabrication halls, dockside
  collection routes, balanced thumper-and-gathering production, and continuous
  casting with additional steel storage. The three thermal envelopes now have
  distinct benefits: insulation; insulation with reduced cold illness; and
  insulation with wider heat zones.
- Moved 16 upgrades earlier and reduced their research prices and durations.
  Timber surveys are available at tier 0; sawmill tooling, gathering logistics
  and hunting logistics at tier I; scout bearings, Outpost bearings, mining core
  mounts and improved scrap recovery at tier II. Scout overhauls and several
  medical, growing and insulation improvements are now tier III.
- Strengthened late-game research. Stormproof Industrial Districts grants +3
  insulation and 100% workplace efficiency to Coal Mines, Steelworks and Wall
  Drills. Deep-Seam Extraction grants 150% Coal Mine workplace efficiency.
  Deep Coal Bins grants 150% coal capacity and Provision Cells grants 200% food
  capacity. Final generator upgrades combine major fuel savings with improved
  stress management. Civic Storm Shelters grants +2 insulation to civic services.
- Organized added research into Engineering, Industry, City Life and Logistics.
  Rows use seven widely spaced lanes. Every supported scenario is checked for
  icon collisions, crossing prerequisite lines, overlapping lines, and lines
  through unrelated icons. Cross-tab prerequisites are named in each tooltip.
  Workshop progression stays together in Engineering. A display at least
  1920 pixels wide is recommended for the expanded tab bar.
- 209 research definitions are active across the supported modes. A New Home
  has 131 added definitions, The Last Autumn 63, and On The Edge 84. The previous
  station recipes and the Gathering Post construction-crash hotfix are preserved.

Save before updating, close Frostpunk, uninstall the previous version, then
install this release. Existing technology GUIDs are preserved, but broader
campaign testing of the revised balance is still needed. Back up saves when
trying a beta overhaul.

Validation: 17 automated installer/research tests; independent parsing of all
408 technology records and 770 modifiers; native research/modifier preservation;
eight scenario membership and geometry audits; patch reconstruction; and a
full archive install/restore check; and a local startup smoke test in which all
six required configurations loaded with zero serialization or decompression
errors. An optional icon-atlas layout regression test guards the Last Autumn
safety-modifier serialization.

Download the **Windows ZIP**, extract it, and run `Install.cmd`. The release ZIP
contains the standalone executable; the GitHub source archive requires Python.
