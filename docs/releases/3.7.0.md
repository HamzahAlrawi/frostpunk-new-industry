# New Industry 3.7.0 beta

A progression overhaul for Windows Steam Frostpunk 1.6.2, including renewable Steam Cores, 216 active research definitions across supported modes, alternate station recipes, and scenario-specific progression.

## Download and install

Download **Frostpunk-New-Industry-3.7.0-Windows.zip**, extract it, close Frostpunk, then run **Install.cmd**. The ZIP includes a standalone Windows executable and the full source. The installer discovers Steam libraries and keeps verified original-archive backups. **Uninstall.cmd** restores them.

The installer requires exact compatible original archive hashes and refuses files modified by other archive mods. Backups and logs are stored under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FrostpunkNewIndustry\`.

## Latest research

- Outpost Network Charters adds two expedition slots per Outpost Depot.
- Forager Supply Web expands The Last Autumn's forager network.
- Conservative Surgical Protocols reduces Radical Treatment amputation risk by 75% at Medical Posts.
- Storm Duty Clothing halves illness chance from cold.
- District heating networks increase heat-zone radii by 50%.
- Prefab Assembly Teams reduces building construction time by 75%.

The release also includes all earlier station recipes and progression upgrades, including Reclaimed Reloading Parts in The Last Autumn.
