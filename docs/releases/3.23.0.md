# New Industry 3.23.0 beta

- **The Last Signal: Faultline** is a new five-act campaign on Switchback Basin. Use the Generator's actual stress gauge to send timed steam pulses. Route heat between homes, clinics and workplaces. A circuit trip chills the city until you bring stress back down.
- Eleven plans have different city requirements, pressure sequences and lasting consequences. Your refit, scout survey and welding decisions shape the routes available in the final whiteout. Jobs allow five to eight days, and a missed job moves the story forward. There are three endings.
- Start Faultline from **Select Scenario**. Existing Winter Road and other Last Signal saves retain their campaign data. The Beacon opens Frostland and reveals three salvage sites in Faultline.
