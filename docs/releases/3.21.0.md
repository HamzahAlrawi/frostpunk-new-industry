# New Industry 3.21.0 beta

## Switchback Basin

- Start **The Last Signal: Winter Road** from **Select Scenario** to play in Switchback Basin. It uses the rock formations and terrain from Endless Points with a fixed starting variation and ten relocated wood, coal, and steel piles.
- Completing a Beacon opens the Frostland map and places three salvage destinations within scout range. Scouts can bring their finds back to the city. For the chapter IV survey plan, send the team to a site, explore it, then order the team home.
- Older Winter Road saves keep their original city and Frostland map. The update checks for a completed Beacon and reveals routes on that existing map, even if the Beacon was built before the update.
- The installer builds the two new scenes from the original Steam archives. It also backs up and restores the scene archive pair.

The build checks both scenario bindings, starter resource placements, the Beacon event against native serialization, and scout destination creation and reveal in the scenario model. The package audit checks all 43 patched entries and restores all eight original archive files.

The installed build reached the main menu with all 13 required configurations loaded and zero serialization or inflate errors. The live check ended at the scenario selector. The new map load and scout round trip still need in-game verification.
