# New Industry 3.18.0 beta — Lifeline

**The Last Signal: Lifeline** replaces service-shift counters with a refugee rescue campaign. The first decision admits eight workers and four engineers and delivers route-specific supplies. Build the reception facilities, keep the Generator running and finish the rescue before the deadline. Successful rescues bring actual people and cargo into the city; failed rescues bring only stragglers and a report of what happened.

The two early convoys compete for one guide rope. You can shelter the injured or repair the winch, then choose an open route for families or a ferry for the repair crews. An optional second rope lets you attempt both crossings if both earlier rescues succeeded. The scenario has its own forecast and three deadline windows: three, five and six days. Supply costs are one-time payments.

[Lifeline player guide](../last-signal.md). Start a new city to play it. Existing Last Signal saves retain the old story. Endless campaigns, laws and research are unchanged.

The serialized-data audit walks all 12 successful routes, three missed rescues, duplicate or stale actions, payment checks, and isolation from existing scenarios and saves. Full campaign and balance playthroughs remain beta work.

All 66 public tests passed. The installer reconstructed 39 patched entries and restored all six original archives in a round-trip test. The local install matched the verified archive hashes. Interactive checks of the opening arrival and first rescue were skipped.
