# Faultline opening art

Three original frames were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The film editor crops, animates and captions those frames and synthesizes its soundscape. Run `python builder/render_faultline_film.py` with FFmpeg on PATH and the Windows Georgia fonts installed to rebuild the film.

## basin.jpg

Original cinematic game concept frame for a Victorian Arctic industrial survival story, landscape 16:9 with crop-safe centered framing. Hand-painted historical realism with fine film grain, slate-blue snow, near-black weathered iron, restrained amber lamplight. Real, worn materials. No words, typography, watermarks, frames, or game UI. A vast high Arctic basin at blue hour, viewed from a snowy ridge. At the bottom, one modest original cylindrical riveted heating tower with a warm furnace window, a few sparse timber cabins, and a single thick iron steam main leaving town on stone piers in the foreground. Low pale cloud, a grounded snowy horizon. Stage the pipe as a clear leading line from tower to foreground, and leave a dark quiet band along the bottom for subtitles.
